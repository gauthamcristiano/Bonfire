Privacy Policy

Bonfire is a private place for people to talk and keep shared memories. We collect only what the app needs to work, we never sell your data, and we never use private messages, photos or Sparks for advertising.

What we collect

  • Account: your name, email address and profile photo from your Google account, and the unique Bonfire ID and username you choose.
  • Content you create: messages, invitations (Pigeons), Sparks and everything added to a Spark's Circle (notes, photos, voice notes, videos), mixtapes you hand over, and your privacy choices (who can find you).
  • Safety information: people you block and reports you send.
  • On your device: Keepsakes, letters, time capsules and fireplace entries are stored on your own device unless a feature says it is shared.
  • Notifications: if you allow them, your browser or phone is asked to show alerts.

How it is used

To sign you in, deliver your messages and Sparks to the people you chose, show you who you are connected to, keep the service safe, and fix problems. Content is shown only to the people you shared it with.

Who else processes data

  • Google Firebase (sign-in and database) stores your account and content.
  • Cloudflare (file storage and the gateway that protects it), when switched on, stores photos, voice notes and videos in a private bucket that is not publicly accessible.
  • Apple iTunes Search receives the song name you type when you search for a song for a mixtape. Song previews are played from Apple's servers. No account details are sent.
  • Links to full songs open YouTube in your browser, which is then governed by YouTube's own policy.

We do not sell personal data and do not share it with advertisers.

How long we keep it

Until you delete it or delete your account. Reports are kept as long as needed to keep Bonfire safe.

Your rights and choices

  • Visibility: choose Everyone, Selected people or Nobody in the Me tab.
  • Download your data: Me, then Safety and your data, then Download my data.
  • Delete your account: Me, then Delete my account. It removes your profile, username, messages and the Sparks you started. You can also request deletion on the account deletion page.
  • Block and report: from any chat, tap the three dots.
  • Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act you may ask us to access, correct or erase your personal data, and to resolve a complaint. Write to the contact address above. If we do not resolve it you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Access is restricted by database rules so that only the people a thing was shared with can read it, and private files are only released after we check who is asking. Messages are not end-to-end encrypted, which means the service operator could technically access stored content. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affecting you happens we will tell you without delay.

Age

Bonfire is for people 18 and older. If you believe a younger person is using it, tell us and we will remove the account.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will tell you in the app before it applies.

Terms of Use

  1. Who can use Bonfire. You must be 18 or older and able to agree to these terms.
  2. Your account. Keep your sign-in secure. You are responsible for what happens through your account.
  3. Your content. You own what you post. You allow Bonfire to store it and show it to the people you chose, only so the service works. Only share things you have the right to share, and never share someone's private photos or details without their consent.
  4. Behave. Follow the Community guidelines below. We may remove content, limit features or close accounts that break them.
  5. Songs. Mixtapes list songs and play short previews provided by Apple. Bonfire does not host or sell music.
  6. No guarantees. Bonfire is provided as it is, and may change or have downtime. Keep your own copy of anything precious; use Download my data.
  7. Ending. You can delete your account at any time. We may suspend accounts that put others at risk.
  8. Law. These terms are governed by the laws of India.

Community guidelines

  • Be kind. No harassment, threats, bullying or hate toward people or groups.
  • No sexual content involving anyone under 18, ever. We report it to the authorities.
  • No nudity or sexual content shared without the clear consent of everyone in it.
  • No violence, self-harm encouragement, or illegal activity.
  • Do not pretend to be someone else.
  • No spam, scams or repeated unwanted invitations.
  • Respect privacy: do not share other people's messages, photos or personal details without permission.

Use the Report option in any chat or on any Spark contribution. Reports are reviewed by a person, and content or accounts that break these rules may be removed. If you or someone you know is in danger, contact local emergency services first.
