Delete your Bonfire account

Bonfire, a private app for friends and shared memories.

The fastest way: inside the app

  1. Open Bonfire and sign in.
  2. Go to the Me tab.
  3. Scroll to Safety and your data and choose Delete my account.
  4. Type DELETE and confirm with Google.

If you cannot sign in

Email REPLACE: your email address from the address you used to sign up, with the subject "Delete my Bonfire account". We will delete the account within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your profile, username, Bonfire ID and privacy settings.
  • Your messages and invitations.
  • Every Spark you started, and the photos, voice notes and videos you added to Sparks.
  • Your sign-in with Google (your Google account itself is not affected).

What may remain

Reports about abuse may be kept as long as needed to keep others safe. Copies of messages that you sent to other people may no longer be removable from their own downloads or backups.
